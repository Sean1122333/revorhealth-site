Our Story

Bridging Healthcare
Across Continents

Founded in 2017 in Bengaluru, MedBridge Global was born from a simple idea: world-class healthcare operations shouldn't be limited by geography.

🏢
MedBridge HQ, Bengaluru
The Beginning

From a 10-Person Team to 1,200+ Professionals

Our founders — a physician and a healthcare IT veteran — saw an opportunity to combine India's deep talent pool with the growing demand for affordable healthcare back-office operations in the US.

Starting with medical coding for three small clinics in Texas, we grew into a full-service healthcare BPO handling revenue cycle management, clinical analytics, telehealth support, and health IT services for over 120 organizations across 32 US states.
